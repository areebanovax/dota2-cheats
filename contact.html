Email

We do not monitor a public inbox for product support. If you need help with a purchase or installation, open a ticket on Discord with your order email and a short description of the issue.

What to include in a ticket

  • Your order email or transaction reference from zadeyo checkout.
  • Windows version (10 or 11, 64-bit).
  • Whether HVCI, TPM, and Secure Boot are enabled.
  • Screenshots or error text if something fails during install.
