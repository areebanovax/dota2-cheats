    Stop Copying Pro Builds in Pub Queue

    Your pos 4 is not Cr1t. Your offlane is not ATF. The replay parser doesn't care that a build worked in a DreamLeague game you'll never play in.

    I bought Vanguard on Timber at minute four because a pro did it in a game where his team had perfect lane setup and a coordinated trilane. I had a Pudge who missed twelve hooks and ate my creeps. Different game. Same item. Bad result.
