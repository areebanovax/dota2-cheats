Cooldown tracking beats meta hero hopping

Spent half a season forcing meta picks I was bad at. Switched to watching spells instead of tabs. Climbed hundreds of MMR. Not a joke.

Why tier lists feel productive and lose games

Everyone told me to play Mars offlane because it was "strong this patch." I went 4-11. The hero was fine. I had no idea when their mid had Blink, or when Oracle had False Promise again. I was playing tabs, not timers.

Hero tier lists make you feel productive. Cooldown tracking makes you win fights. Those are not the same hobby. Check patch notes on dota2.com, then ignore the urge to force a hero you cannot play under pressure.

The three-spell rule

Keep a mental clock on three abilities max per game. Usually: mid ultimate, their hardest disable, and one save. That is enough to change how you take rune fights and high ground.

  1. Name the three spells in draft or loading screen.
  2. Call them out when they are used, even if only in your head.
  3. Play aggressive in the window after they are down.
  4. Respect the window when they come back up.
  5. Reset the list if a core swaps roles or buys a new save item.

Once I treated BKB like a 90-second resource instead of a vibe, fights stopped being random coin flips.

Item timings belong on the same clock

Spells are not the only timers. Blink, BKB, Force Staff, and Gleipnir change fight geometry. Inventory awareness from our Hero ESP guide pairs with cooldown tracking: if their mid just used ultimate and has no BKB, the dive is different than when both are ready.

Lane stage: what to track at 0-10

Early game, track the spells that kill you in lane: mid nuke, offlane stun, support save. Do not track everything. You will forget everything. One accurate timer beats five approximate ones.

If you support, your job includes calling "no stun" when their offlaner burns it on creeps. Mid players should say when opposing mid ultimate is down after a failed rune fight.

Mid and late game: fight windows

High ground fails when you walk into ready ultimates. Before you commit, ask: is their big disable available? Is their save available? Is BKB available on the hero you need to kill first? If you cannot answer, wait for vision and information. Our ward vision guide covers how to get that information without feeding.

Smoke into a ready Black Hole is not bravery. It is a free highlight for them.

Practice drill for one week

Pick three games a day. Write the three spells before first blood. After the game, score yourself: how many times did you play the window correctly? Do not track MMR for that week. Track honesty.

Public match data on Dotabuff can show death timing patterns. It will not track whether you knew a cooldown was down. That part is on you.
