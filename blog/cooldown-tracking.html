Cooldown tracking beats meta hero hopping

Spent half a season forcing meta picks I was bad at. Switched to watching spells instead of tabs. Climbed hundreds of MMR. Not a joke.

Meta picks without spell knowledge

Everyone told me to play Mars offlane because it was “strong this patch.” I went 4–11. The hero was fine. I had no idea when their mid had Blink, or when Oracle had False Promise again. I was playing tabs, not timers.

Hero tier lists make you feel productive. Cooldown tracking makes you win fights. Those are not the same hobby.

The three-spell rule

Keep a mental clock on three abilities max per game. Usually: mid ultimate, their hardest disable, and one save. That is enough to change how you take rune fights and high ground.

  1. Name the three spells in draft or loading screen.
  2. Call them out when they are used — even if only in your head.
  3. Play aggressive in the window after they are down.
  4. Respect the window when they come back up.

Once I treated BKB like a 90-second resource instead of a vibe, my fights stopped being random coin flips.

Information vs hero pool

My hero pool got smaller. Results got better. Tools like the cooldown tracker on DOTA2 Cheats features exist because the gap between “I see the hero” and “I know what they can cast in eight seconds” is enormous in pubs.

Wish someone said this at 1k MMR instead of linking another tier list.
