Hero ESP for ranked pubs

Hero ESP removes fog blackouts. Used well, it cuts surprise deaths and improves dives, farms, and rotations. Used poorly, it is just more noise on screen.

What Hero ESP actually changes in pubs

Hero ESP shows enemy heroes through fog with level and item context. In ranked pubs, that means fewer "where did he come from" deaths and better decisions about diving, farming, and rotating. It does not auto-win games. It removes information blackouts.

Pair ESP with cooldown tracking and map habits. Seeing a hero is useless if you still walk into their ready ultimate.

Lane stage uses

Know when supports leave. Know when the offlaner walks behind you. Know whether mid is missing with a bottle refill path. ESP shortens the delay between "something feels wrong" and "I should back."

Mid game uses

Track who is farming where. Avoid walking into three heroes "randomly" stacked in your jungle. Time your rotations when enemy cores are showing on the opposite side of the map.

Late game uses

High ground and Roshan need accurate hero locations. Combine ESP with the Roshan guide and vision guide. Objectives fail when you guess.

Practice without relying on the overlay forever

Use ESP for a week while writing down what you would have missed. Then play a few games focusing on the same habits with less reliance. The goal is better decisions, not a permanent crutch you never understand.
